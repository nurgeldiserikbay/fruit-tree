**App name:** Fruit Tree

## Full description (EN)

Welcome to Fruit Tree — a peaceful 3D fruit-picking experience.

Rotate each tree, look between its branches and gently collect ripe fruit at your own pace. Clear every fruit to unlock the next tree, discover different fruit species, and revisit trees you've already completed whenever you want.

- Explore trees in 3D with intuitive touch controls.
- Enjoy calm sounds and a warm nature-inspired atmosphere.
- Collect every fruit to unlock the next tree.
- Restore previously completed trees and play again.
- Play without timers, lives or pressure.
- Save your orchard progress on your device.

Take your time. The garden can wait.
